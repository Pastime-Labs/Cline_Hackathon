# Cline AI Builder Hackathon - Round 2

## Source and use

This file preserves the relevant information from the one-page problem statement, Cline_AI_Builder_Hackathon_PS.pdf. Use it as the source of truth for the hackathon brief and submission requirements in this workspace. The original PDF is being replaced by this Markdown file at the user's request.

## Challenge

Choose one real problem faced by Indian society and build an AI-powered solution in **8 hours**. Deliver a working product that a real person could use tomorrow; a slide deck alone does not meet the challenge.

Use **Cline as your AI coding partner from the first commit to the last**. You may also integrate Cline into the product itself, for example through the **Cline SDK**. Integration inside the product is optional and earns bonus points.

The problem is yours to choose. Healthcare, education, agriculture, governance, finance, safety, and climate are examples, not an exhaustive list or a restriction on the domain.

## Context and illustrative problems

The brief emphasizes everyday problems affecting people across Indian languages, incomes, and literacy levels:

- Farmers may discover crop problems only after damage has occurred.
- Patients in small towns may travel for hours to receive only a few minutes with a doctor.
- Students may have to study notes written in a language they do not think in.
- Citizens may lose months because they never learn about government schemes they qualify for.

It highlights AI capabilities such as understanding Indian languages and voice, reading messy documents, reasoning over local data, and reaching people with basic smartphones. These are opportunities described in the brief, not mandatory features for every submission.

One example solution is an AI-enabled car dashcam that detects potholes while driving and automatically prepares a complaint. This is an illustration, not an assigned project.

## Deliverables

### Public GitHub codebase with README

Provide a public repository with a clear README covering:

- The problem.
- The solution.
- Architecture.
- Technology stack.
- Setup steps.
- How Cline was used during the build.

### Presentation (PPT)

Provide a presentation covering:

- The problem and solution.
- Architecture.
- AI models and tools involved.
- How Cline was used in the build and inside the solution, where applicable.
- Challenges faced.
- Future scope.

The brief asks the presentation to cover Cline use during the build and inside the solution; the challenge explicitly makes in-product integration optional.

### Demo video - mandatory for every submission

Provide a short walkthrough of the working solution that:

- Demonstrates a real user scenario.
- Shows Cline in action during the build.

### Social media post - optional, for brownie points

Share the build, demo, experience, or a participation shoutout, and tag Cline.

## Evaluation criteria

The solution will be evaluated on:

1. Creativity and uniqueness of the solution.
2. Innovation.
3. Real-world usefulness.
4. Effective use of Cline.
5. Product usability and overall execution.
6. Technical implementation.

**Bonus points:** Use Cline inside the product itself, in addition to using it to build the product.
